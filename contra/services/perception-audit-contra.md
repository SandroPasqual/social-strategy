## Service Description

Copy this into the rich text editor:

```
A precise, written diagnosis of the gap between how your business operates and how it appears publicly.

Not a strategy. Not a visual recommendation. Not a roadmap. A complete account of what the gap is, where it comes from, and what it costs you — documented with enough precision that you know what you're dealing with and what needs to happen next.

You leave with a document. Not a direction. The direction is yours to decide.

How It Works:
1. Questionnaire — You complete a structured form in writing, with the same seriousness you'd bring to a board meeting. Asynchronously, at your pace, without shortcuts.
2. Discovery session — One focused 60-minute call. No small talk. Straight to business reality.
3. Independent research — I analyze your market position, your category, the gap between how you operate and how you appear.
4. Perception Gap Report — Delivered within 7 days. Written. Complete. No open questions.

What You Receive — a single document containing:
- Exactly what your current presence signals to the market
- Where it diverges from the reality of your business
- What that divergence costs you in concrete terms: pricing conversations, client quality, market authority
- The precise gap that needs to be closed — named, located, and documented

What This Is Not: This is not a free consultation repackaged as a product. Not a sample designed to sell you the next thing. Not a mood board, checklist, or competitor analysis. It is a diagnosis, complete in itself.

Includes: Pre-engagement questionnaire, independent market and presence research, one structured discovery session (60 min), Perception Gap Report (delivered within 7 days).
```
