# Post-Session Notes

> **Internal** — Documented immediately after the discovery session, before doing anything else.

## Capture These Four Things

1. **The moment the client hesitated**
2. **The exact words they used repeatedly**
3. **What they avoided answering directly**
4. **The first contradiction between the form and what they said on the call**

---

These are the nerves of the report. Not the information — the reactions.
