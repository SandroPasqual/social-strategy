# Independent Research

> **Internal** — Raw material. Not presented to the client.

Conducted in two stages. The first before the discovery session. The second after.

---

## Stage 1 — Surface Research

> Conduct before the discovery session. Time limit: 45 minutes.

**Purpose:** Understand what the outside world sees before you speak to the client. Enter the session knowing what their public presence signals — not to form conclusions, but to ask better questions.

**What to cover:**

- **Website** — What category does it place them in? What does the headline claim? What is missing from the first 10 seconds of reading?
- **LinkedIn** — Personal profile and company page if it exists. How do they describe themselves? What do they emphasize? What do endorsements and recommendations reveal that the self-description doesn't?
- **Any other public touchpoints** — Press mentions, podcast appearances, published writing, social presence. What impression accumulates across all of them?

**What to capture:**

One short paragraph — not a list. Write what impression forms if you know nothing about this person except what you just read. That paragraph goes into the discovery session as context, not as conclusion.

**What not to do:**

Do not form a diagnosis yet. Do not decide where the gap is. Surface research tells you what to look for in the session — nothing more.

---

## Stage 2 — Deep Research

> Conduct after the discovery session and before writing the report.

**Purpose:** Now you know what the client told you — the tensions, the contradictions, the things they avoided. Use this to search with direction, not in general.

**What to cover:**

- **Category signals** — How do competitors position themselves? What language does the market use? Where does the client's presence sit relative to others in the same space — above, below, or sideways?
- **Specific gaps identified in the session** — If the client mentioned a situation where their presence cost them an opportunity, look for the evidence of why. What does the outside world see that would produce that outcome?
- **Involuntary associations** — What category, level, or type of business does their presence align them with — intentionally or not? Who else looks like this, and is that the right company to be in?
- **What the right clients would find** — If the client's ideal client searched for them right now, what would they find? Would it make them more or less likely to reach out?

**What to capture:**

Notes organized by what you found, not by where you looked. The source doesn't matter — the signal does.

---

## What This Feeds

Stage 1 feeds the discovery session — specifically the questions in minutes 5-25 and 25-45.

Stage 2 feeds Section 1 and Section 3 of the Perception Gap Report — what the presence currently signals, and where the gap lives.

The report is written after Stage 2 is complete. Not before.
