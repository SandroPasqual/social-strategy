# Discovery Session — Structure

> **Perception Audit** — Internal operating document.

**Duration:** 60 minutes. No more.

---

## Pre-Call Preparation

Read the client's completed questionnaire the day before. Mark:

- Places where the response is vague or evasive
- Tensions they mentioned but did not develop
- Contradictions between sections
- What they did not say at all

The session does not repeat the questionnaire. It goes straight into the marked areas.

---

## Minute 0-5 — Calibration

A single opening question:

> "Reading what you wrote, I identified a few places where I'd like to go deeper. Before we do that — is there anything you wanted to write in the form but didn't?"

Listen to what they say. And what they don't say.

---

## Minute 5-25 — Business Reality

Dive into the contradictions and gaps from Section 1 of the questionnaire.

Depth questions — used selectively, not all of them:

> "You said you're difficult to replace because of X. When a client does leave — what actually happened?"

> "Describe the best client you've ever had. What made them the best?"

> "Is there something in your business that you consider obvious and that others treat as remarkable?"

> "What have you refused to do, even though the market would have paid you for it?"

---

## Minute 25-45 — The Gap and Its Cost

Dive into the tensions from Sections 2 and 3 of the questionnaire.

> "You mentioned [situation X from the form]. What exactly happened in that moment — what did you feel was missing?"

> "The conversation you have repeatedly that shouldn't be necessary — who usually initiates it? The client or you?"

> "If tomorrow your public presence perfectly reflected what you've built — what would be the first concrete thing that would change?"

> "Is there someone in your market whose public presence you respect? What do they do differently?"

---

## Minute 45-55 — Decision Context

> "Why now and not 2 years ago?"

> "What would it mean for you to complete this audit and do nothing with it?"

The second question is important. The answer shows you whether the client is serious or whether they bought themselves the peace of mind that "they did something."

---

## Minute 55-60 — Closing

A single closing sentence:

> "I have everything I need. You will receive the document in 7 days."

No promises about what it will contain. No preview. No questions about whether they have anything else to add — they had 55 minutes.
