# Goodspell — Brand Strategy Diagnosis

A complete Perception Audit for a brand strategy practice. The business had built a proprietary methodology across 30+ indexed documents, with a custom client portal and a complete visual system. The public presence — a website in draft, a paused LinkedIn page — communicated at the level of a solo operator just getting started.

**Process:** Pre-engagement questionnaire → Discovery session (60 min) → Independent market research → Perception Gap Report

**Output:** A 5-section report identifying the gap between the rigor of the internal system and the thinness of the public presence, with the precise cost of that divergence and the one priority that must be solved first. Four supporting documents showing the full audit process.

**Result:** A complete public presence built from the diagnosis — profile, service listing, consistent voice across channels.
